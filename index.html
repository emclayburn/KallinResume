About

I'm a recent BYU grad with a degree in Journalism and Sports Media, and I've built my experience around one simple idea: sports are better with a good story attached. During school, I wrote for The Daily Universe, BYU's student newspaper, while also working across broadcast production and social content — calling games, cutting highlight reels, and finding the angle that makes a story worth reading.

I like moving fast, working with a team, and figuring out the right format for a story depending on where it's going to live — an article reads different than a highlight reel, and I care about getting both right. I'm looking for a role where I can keep telling stories, whatever form that takes.
